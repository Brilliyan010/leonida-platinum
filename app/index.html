    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). One playthrough, no online trophies, no mission replay — so play in this order and save often.

    1. 1

      Story playthrough

      Chapters 1–5, all story missions and stranger finales as they appear. Scan vehicles with WAiNK and study animals through binoculars as you go — the 100% checklist counts them already.

    2. 2

      One-time moments

      The single missable and the story-adjacent one-offs: meditate at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, then the partner, robbery and stunt moments while the chances are in front of you.

    3. 3

      Collectibles & exploration

      Card sets, WAiNK spots, vehicles, areas of Leonida, garages and the Scooter Bros registrations. Everything stays available in free roam after the story.

    4. 4

      Activities & grinds

      Shooting Range golds, drone races, stunts, races, fishing, arcade, bowling and the 80 challenges — 70 of 80 count toward 100%, so skip the hardest ten.

    5. 5

      Cleanup & platinum

      Finish the 100% Completion checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion), mop up what is left, and the platinum pops with the 54th other trophy.

    Points of no return

    • End of Chapter 1 — the one missable. Positive Mental Attitude: after the mission Down to Earth, Lucia meditates at Jason’s house. Do that balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends.
    • Character-only questlines. Andy’s missions are Lucia-only; Tyrone’s and Bret’s are Jason-only. Switch characters and finish each strand before the story ends.
    • Irredeemable profile. A profile choice that cannot be undone — decide before you commit to it; it is not needed for any trophy.
    • No mission replay. Story missions cannot be replayed, so a story trophy is earned by playing the mission, not by redoing it. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter (15 manual slots).
    • Hourglass quests expire. Timed side quests can lapse, but they do not count toward any trophy.
