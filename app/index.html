Roadmap to the platinum

One playthrough, about 200 hours, difficulty 4/10 (PowerPyx). No online trophies, no difficulty settings, no mission replay — but free-roam stays open after the story, and only one trophy can be missed. Work the stages in order.

Points of no return — read this first

  • 1 missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. The Chapter 1 meditation at Jason’s house (after the mission Down to Earth) can only be done in Chapter 1. Meditate there before the chapter ends, and keep a manual save at the end of Chapter 1.
  • No mission replay. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter — the game offers 15 manual slots plus autosave.
  • Cheats disable trophies (and saving). Keep a trophy run completely cheat-free.
  • Character-only questlines: Andy’s missions are Lucia-only; Tyrone’s and Bret’s are Jason-only. The last meditation needs both characters, after Coffee Break in Chapter 2.
  • Irredeemable profile cannot be undone; quests marked with an hourglass expire (they do not count for trophies).
  • After the story the 100% checklist unlocks at Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Everything in it stays available in free-roam.
